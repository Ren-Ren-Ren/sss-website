INTRO
Studio Stone Soup began in a Loft Studio in Brooklyn — 

Centered on new ideas and defining experiences across image and motion in film, commercial, music, and fashion. 
We partner with brands, agencies, and artists to develop and produce story-driven work with a cinematic lens and strong cultural perspective. From development through final delivery, ensuring creative and execution move seamlessly together.

Based in New York, working across and beyond.

We believe the strongest work starts before the camera does — in the idea, the instinct, the thing worth saying. Everything after that is craft.
