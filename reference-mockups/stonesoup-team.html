Studio

Studio Stone Soup began in a rented room in Bushwick — four walls and good light, open to whoever asked.

What we learned running that space is that production, direction, and branding hold up better at the same table than handed between vendors. That's still how we work now, whether the job is a single campaign or a system built to outlast it.

We're based in Brooklyn, working across New York and beyond.
