Project Description

Nette came to us with a fragrance and a feeling: luxury that doesn't announce itself. Our job was to build the world that scent lives in — three locations, thirty stills, three social videos, and an influencer layer, all shot across two days in New York.

Cary Leeds wasn't a backdrop, it was a requirement — the spectator stand made a specific broadcast-style shot possible, one no other court in the city could offer. The apartment interior leaned on window light and restraint instead of styling, closer to an Architectural Digest spread than a beauty campaign.

We ran it lean on purpose: hair, makeup, and styling folded into the creative fee, crew transport coordinated with the client, and a producer dedicated entirely to locations and permits. Lean doesn't mean loose — every risk on a multi-location, multi-day shoot gets named and planned for before call time, not during it.
